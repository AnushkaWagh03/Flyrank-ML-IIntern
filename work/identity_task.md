## Style Note

**Fonts:** Space Grotesk for headings and Inter for body text.
**Colors:** `#2563EB`, `#111827`, `#F9FAFB`, and `#7C3AED`.

The visual mood is clean, technical, and minimal. The design should frame the work and evidence clearly without competing for attention.

---

## Design Principles

* Use only two fonts consistently.
* Keep the color palette limited to four colors.
* Use whitespace to improve readability.
* Avoid unnecessary gradients and decorative elements.
* Use real project screenshots and technical diagrams where possible.
* Ensure visuals provide evidence rather than decoration.
* Keep the interface clear, structured, and easy to scan.
